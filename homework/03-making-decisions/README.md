# Making Decisions

This is independent, ungraded practice after the matching in-class activity. Follow the [track setup](../README.md).

## Your task

Build a parcel-price checker. Read a whole-number weight in grams: 1 through 500 costs $4, 501 through 1000 costs $7, and above 1000 costs $10. Zero and negative weights are invalid. Use conditional branches to print exactly one outcome. Assume input converts to an integer.

## Check your work

- Weights 1, 500, 501, 1000, and 1001 produce $4, $4, $7, $7, and $10.
- Zero and negative weights produce an error.

Keep your edited starter files and add a prediction, an actual result, and a question or discovery to notes.md. Nothing here is submitted for marks.
