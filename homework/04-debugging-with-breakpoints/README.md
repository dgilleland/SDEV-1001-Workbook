# Debugging with Breakpoints

This is independent, ungraded practice after the matching in-class activity. Follow the [track setup](../README.md).

## Your task

Investigate the intentionally incorrect mean calculation in main.py. Predict the result, set a breakpoint, and step through while inspecting the variables. Record what differs from your prediction before fixing it. Use the debugger rather than relying only on extra print calls. After correcting the expression, try three equal values.

## Check your work

- The mean of 10, 20, and 30 is 20.
- The mean of 5, 5, and 5 is 5.
- Your notes explain the mistake and how stepping revealed it.

Keep your edited starter files and add a prediction, an actual result, and a question or discovery to notes.md. Nothing here is submitted for marks.
