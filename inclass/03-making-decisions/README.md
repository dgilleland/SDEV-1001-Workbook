# Making Decisions

Let's explore this topic together. Follow the [track setup](../README.md), then open the supplied starter files.

## Explore together

1. Read an age as an integer. Assume valid whole-number input.
2. Use if, elif, and else to choose admission: under 6 costs $0, ages 6 through 17 cost $5, and ages 18 and over cost $10.
3. Give a negative age an error outcome instead of a price.
4. Try values immediately below and at each boundary. Explain why branch order matters.

## Check your work

- Ages 5, 6, 17, and 18 produce $0, $5, $5, and $10.
- A negative age produces an error.
